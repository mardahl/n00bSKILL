# Synopsis and problemformulering

Use when starting a report, narrowing a broad topic, or building a `synopsis`.

## Narrowing path

`emne` -> problem -> affected audience -> `problemformulering` -> `afgrænsning`.

Reject `noget om ...` tasks, yes/no questions, vague questions, and unbounded questions. Convert the topic into one exact, answerable question.

Add a `hypotese` or `arbejdshypotese` when the report investigates causes, explanations, or solutions. Distinguish `hypotese`, `antagelse`, and `teori`; define what evidence could support, weaken, or reject the hypothesis.

## 9-part synopsis template

Planning gate before drafting. Each point is 1-3 complete sentences, not keywords:

1. `Mit emne er ...`
2. `Det er et "problem", at ...`
3. `Det er et "problem" for ...`
4. `Jeg vil undersøge, om "problemet" skyldes ...`
5. `Jeg vil derfor finde svaret på dette spørgsmål ...`
6. `Rapporten skal kunne bruges af ... til at ...`
7. `Jeg vil kun beskæftige mig med ... fordi ...`
8. `Jeg vil bygge rapporten på ...`
9. `Jeg vil gøre sådan-og-sådan ...`

Do not copy this future-looking method text into the final report. The final `metodeafsnit` says what was actually done.
